# Add decorrelated points beyond 2.5D attention-MIL

Three ideas clear the +0.005 macro-AUC-per-few-days bar on evidence, and all three attack different failure modes of the CoAtNet-rmlp-2 384px 2.5D-window attention-MIL baseline, which matters because the stated goal is 0.969 by 22 Oct 2026 with errors decorrelated from the public blend (within-class rho ≤ ~0.8). The cheapest win is stochastic top-K instance masking plus multi-branch attention (ACMIL-lite / MIL-Dropout), which repeatedly adds +2–4pp AUC on pathology bags and directly counters the documented attention-collapse mode where the top-10 instances hold >0.85 of bag mass ([ACMIL](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.07125v4), [MIL-Dropout](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.14783v1)). The second is a per-finding Query2Label-style decoder with at most three sparse clinical edges and label-mask training, porting the +0.8 to +3 mAP multi-label head gains to the 12-finding head where small focal lesions (lateral meniscus, cartilage/OA) gain most from per-label pooling ([Query2Label](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10834), [C-Tran](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.14027)). The third is weighted cross-plane per-finding cross-attention fused jointly with a global-plus-ROI cross-scale stream, the only pattern with both knee-MRI (+1.0pp multi-view, +2.2% MRPyrNet ACL) and pathology cross-scale (+4.9pp) measurements behind it ([Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/bioengineering-and-biotechnology/articles/10.3389/fbioe.2023.1268543/full), [PMLR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v143/dunnhofer21a/dunnhofer21a.pdf), [CS-MIL](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11016375/)). Domain pretraining (RadImageNet) and CNN–transformer pairing are held as the decorrelation hedge, not a top-three pick, because their knee deltas (+4.8% ACL / +4.5% meniscus averaged over 24 scenarios) reverse under different tuning grids ([RadImageNet](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9530758), [CS231N](https://cs231n.stanford.edu/2024/papers/towards-optimal-convolutional-transfer-learning-architectures-fo.pdf)). Every projection to RSNA-2026 macro-AUC below is marked PROJECTION (UNVERIFIED); every measured number is a REPORTED result on another dataset, never RSNA-2026.

## Three bets ranked by expected gain per engineering day clear the bar

No 2021–2026 paper reports a clean macro-AUC delta of a CoAtNet/window-attention-MIL pipeline versus a matched 2.5D CNN plus attention-MIL baseline on knee MRI, so the ranking below combines REPORTED effect sizes elsewhere with engineering cost and decorrelation value for this specific baseline, and the localise → crop → 2.5D CNN → attention/GRU/transformer → ensemble stack itself is taken as known and not re-argued. The ordering logic is gain per day under noisy 12-label supervision with ~100–200 windows per study, with the weakest findings (lateral OA, PF OA, synovitis, lateral meniscus) breaking ties toward methods that help small or plane-specific lesions. Stochastic masking wins on cost because it touches only the aggregator over cached features and needs no new annotations, while the query-decoder and fusion bets cost more days but buy the plane- and scale-diversity that actually decorrelates from a public blend of similar 2.5D models.

The key caveat spanning all three ranks is that every REPORTED number above comes from pathology slides, natural images, or MRNet-era knee sets, never from 12-label RSNA-2026 macro-AUC, and the head-to-head this team actually needs (each idea versus its own frozen CoAtNet attention-MIL control) has to be run in-house. The researchers found zero (correlation, gain) pairs of the form "models correlate at r=X, blend gains +Y" for any medical task, so the rho ≤ 0.8 target cannot be predicted from literature and must be measured as per-finding Pearson/Spearman on out-of-fold predictions with greedy forward blending. Ordinal CORAL/CORN heads, Mamba sequence models, and backbone swaps all fall below the top three because their gains are either theoretically vacuous on pure binary labels, washed out at 100–200-instance bag sizes, or limited to ~1–2% ImageNet-neighbour deltas, as detailed in the catalogue sections that follow.

## Stochastic masking and multi-branch attention tame noisy-bag collapse

The failure mode is measured, not hypothetical: across three pathology datasets the summed top-10 attention values exceed 0.85 of every bag, meaning ten windows dominate each study-level decision, and stochastic top-K masking cuts that concentration from 0.87 to 0.6 on CAMELYON16 ([ACMIL](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.07125v4)). Two independent groups converged on the same prescription — ACMIL's train-only Stochastic Top-K Instance Masking (M=5 branches, K=10, p=0.6–0.8, removed at inference) and MIL-Dropout's top-K DropInstance — and both report that masking the salient minority beats masking at random or masking the bottom, with hard removal at p=1.0 hurting relative to stochastic masking ([ACMIL](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.07125v4), [MIL-Dropout](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.14783v1)). MIL-Dropout is the only source with a mechanism tied to label noise, claiming that only top-K dropping reduces gradient-direction error and flattens minima under Gaussian, Poisson, and salt-and-pepper input-noise attacks, alongside mean classic-MIL gains of +9.7pp and WSI gains of +2.3pp accuracy / +2.48pp AUC on CAMELYON16 averaged over four aggregators and two feature types ([MIL-Dropout](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.14783v1)). The practical consequence the researchers draw is dropout-first: plain ABMIL plus dropout reaches TransMIL/DTFD-class performance, so a full TransMIL stack is deferred unless ABMIL-plus-dropout underfits.

## Per-finding query decoders with three sparse edges fit knee labels

The multi-label head literature is unanimous in direction but narrow in domain: every numeric gain is natural-image mAP, with zero head-to-heads of transformer-decoder heads versus independent per-class queries on any medical task, a gap all three researcher notes flag. Query2Label's learnable-label-as-query decoder reaches 91.3% COCO mAP with its largest margins on small and medium objects (39.5 vs 37.8 small, 77.5 vs 74.2 medium against TResNetL), which is the most transferable argument to meniscal tears and cartilage defects that occupy few voxels ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10834)). ML-Decoder removes decoder self-attention (linear not quadratic in queries) with group decoding that adds only 10–20% FLOPs even at 9,600 classes and loses ~0.1% at group ratio 96, implying negligible overhead at 12 queries, and its finding that fixed queries match learned ones makes random initialisation safe ([WACV](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2023/html/Ridnik_ML-Decoder_Scalable_and_Versatile_Classification_Head_WACV_2023_paper.html), [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.12933)). C-Tran contributes the training mechanism rather than the architecture: masking 25–100% of labels as unknown with ternary state embeddings and predicting masked from unmasked plus image yields +0.8 COCO-80 / +1.0 VG-500 mAP without any word-embedding prior, using only 2–4 layers, at the cost of label-count memory (≤2,000 labels on 4× Titan X, irrelevant at 12) ([arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.14027), [CVPR](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2021/papers/Lanchantin_General_Multi-Label_Image_Classification_With_Transformers_CVPR_2021_paper.pdf)).

## Weighted cross-plane and cross-scale fusion plus domain pretraining decide decorrelation

Late fusion by logistic regression over per-plane probabilities is the MRNet default that every subsequent knee paper attacks, with fitted coefficients already hinting at non-redundant views (axial PD for abnormality/meniscus, coronal T1 for ACL) ([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6258509/)). The pattern that beats it is consistent across sources: Li's shared-weight 3D ResNet with MLP view-weighting and feature concatenation reaches 97.00% MRI-ACL and 92.86% MRNet-v1.0, +1.00/+1.08pp over the best single view with monotonically rising AUC in view count, while plain or equal-contribution concatenation is noise-vulnerable ([Frontiers](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/bioengineering-and-biotechnology/articles/10.3389/fbioe.2023.1268543/full), [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-51888-4)). CoPAS generalises the lesson to joint plane-and-sequence attention — weight-shared ResNet3D-18 encoder, cross-attention where the main plane queries rotated cross-plane volumes, a plane–abnormality matrix whose preferences correlate with radiologists', and 0.812 mean AUC over 12 findings across 1,748 patients (0.721–0.726 externally) beating MRNet/MPFuseNet/ELNet adaptations in 8 of 12, strongest on cruciate ligaments and weakest where single-task MRNet wins on MCL/EFFU ([Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-51888-4)). KneeDINO's per-task cross-plane attention (0.942 ACL / 0.871 meniscus / 0.955 abnormal on MRNet) is the same shape in miniature but preprint-grade with UNVERIFIED year and missing baseline tables ([OpenReview](https://openreview.net/pdf?id=NPNray7bzh)). The negative control is Harman: three-plane hybrid fusion at 0.949 underperforms the single sagittal plane at 0.976 for ACL, proving fusion must be learned and weighted, never averaged ([DergiPark](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/pub/jai/article/1926635)).

## Conclusion

The evidence converges on doing the cheapest anti-collapse regularisation first, the label-structure head second, and the joint plane-and-scale fusion third, because each successive bet costs more days but buys a less correlated error profile, which is the actual constraint against a public blend of lookalike 2.5D models. The highest-risk temptation is the reverse order — chasing backbone swaps, Mamba stacks, or ordinal heads whose REPORTED wins either vanish under retuning or collapse to ordinary logistic units on binary labels. What changes the plan on contact with data is narrow: keep any addition only if it survives its own frozen-control ablation by at least +0.005 val macro-AUC with masking disabled at inference, and promote the RadImageNet-plus-transformer hedge the moment out-of-fold correlations show the three picks converging above rho ~0.8.
